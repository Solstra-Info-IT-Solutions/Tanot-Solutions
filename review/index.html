I measured these in the browser at 1440, 1024 and 390px. The biggest problem is that spacing isn't systematic: every section sets its own padding, and the gaps between sections add up unevenly.

  • One primary per group; the secondary always sits to its right.
  • Title Case labels, with an arrow only on "go somewhere" CTAs.

Cards, forms and images

  • Card: white, 1px #E5E9F0 hairline border, 20px radius, 32px padding, near-flat shadow. Large surfaces (form, CTA) use 28px radius.
  • Split sections: one .split grid with align-items: stretch, so both halves are always the same height.
  • Inputs: 46px tall, 10px radius, label above, 4px soft blue focus ring, inline error text.
  • Icons: 22px line icons inside a 44px tinted tile; 20px checklist ticks.
  • Images: blog 16:10, founder 4:5, awards 96px square, all with object-fit (contain for certificates and logos, cover for photos).
  • Logo walls: a hairline grid of equal 96px cells, logos at 32px max height, greyscale until hover.
3 · Summary of improvements

What changed, section by section

Revision 3 is a ground-up visual redesign with one restrained system: a white base, very light tinted bands, brand blue as the only accent, and one card style. The section order, copy and claims are unchanged.

Header & hero

  • A slim 72px glass header with centred navigation and a single blue CTA.
  • A soft gradient hero with a faint grid, a rating pill, a 56px headline and a floating form card.
  • The four stats now sit in one clean white strip at the bottom of the hero instead of a heavy grey band.

Logos & reviews

  • Client and "featured on" logos sit in hairline logo walls of equal cells, so every logo gets the same space.
  • The video and featured quote form one equal-height row, with two equal cards below. All author rows line up along a divider.

Services

  • A quiet vertical tab list inside a card, beside one panel card of exactly the same height. The panel is sized to the longest service, so switching tabs never shifts the page.
  • Each panel: icon + title, description, 2-column checklist, CTAs pinned to the bottom.

Case study & why us

  • The case study is one card split in two: the dashboard on a tinted panel, and the story, a 3-up metrics row and CTAs on the right.
  • Why us is a centred header with a 3 × 2 grid of equal feature cards and one CTA, with no competing dark panels.

Awards, founder & team

  • Award cards have a fixed 200px image stage (contain-fit) above the title, so certificates, photos and trophies all align.
  • The founder photo matches the height of the story column, with a 3-figure stats row and the signature + CTAs at the bottom.
  • Team cards use initials avatars until the real photos are supplied.

FAQ, blog, CTA & footer

  • FAQ: the intro column matches the accordion's height, with the "Still deciding?" card pinned to the bottom.
  • Blog cards use 16:10 images, a category pill, a 3-line title clamp and the meta row pinned to the bottom.
  • A centred blue CTA band with a subtle grid. The footer has a white logo, 5 tidy columns and a slim legal bar.

6 · Assumptions & open questions

Needs client confirmation

Nothing below was invented. These are gaps found in the live content, plus the small copy edits made for layout and readability.

  1. Lisa Warren transcript: generated with local speech-to-text and lightly edited (brand spelling, one stumble). Please confirm the wording with Lisa before publishing; the raw, timestamped version is in review/lisa-warren-transcript-raw.txt.
  2. Case study screenshot: the current dashboard image is 512px wide, which is why it looks unclear. Please export it again at 1600px or wider.
  3. Duplicate testimonial: the live page shows Sandeep Das's quote twice. The mockup keeps it once, in the row of three written reviews under the video and transcript.
  4. Team photos: all four team images on the live page are empty placeholders, so the mockup uses initials. Please supply portraits (square, at least 400px).
  5. Awards: two different awards are both titled "Best ROI Focused SEO Agency 2026". Please confirm each award's name, issuer and year.
  6. Missing URLs: the "Learn More" links for Technical, AI, Local, SaaS SEO and Content Marketing (only Link Building has a page in the nav), the direct case-study URL and Renu's LinkedIn. These are marked with data-todo in the HTML.
  7. Client logo names: two logos (first tile and the first tile of row 2) have no alt text on the live site and couldn't be identified with confidence.
  8. Copy edits made: sentence-case section headings; "02+" → "2+"; "Roi" → "ROI"; in the case study, "the traffic tanked up" → "traffic climbed" (since "tanked" reads as a drop); "Email" → "Work email" with placeholders; a one-line Clutch proof under the hero CTAs ("5.0 rating on Clutch", taken from the existing badge).
  9. Colour shifts: the stats band goes from grey to footer navy, and the header CTA from black to brand blue. Both reuse colours already on the site.
  10. Footer logo: the mockup turns the existing logo white with a CSS filter. Please supply an official reversed (white) logo file for production.
  11. Content placements: the final CTA reuses existing claims (no upfront payment, transparent process, white-hat links) and the existing "free audit" offer. The founder stats reuse 11+ years, founded 2020 and 150+ clients. The hero pill reuses the Clutch 5.0 rating and 11+ years, and links to clutch.co/profile/tanot-solutions (the URL used on the live site). The hero proof points reuse existing claims. Three short labels are new: "Real results from real campaigns" (case study), the form button "Get My Free Strategy" and "Not sure which service fits your goals?" (services). The footer lists all six homepage services; five link to the services section until their pages exist.
  12. Prototype limits: images are hotlinked from tanotsolutions.com, the lead form only validates (it isn't wired to WPForms), and the reviews video is the live Vimeo embed.
